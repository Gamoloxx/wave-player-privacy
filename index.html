Privacy Policy: Wave Player

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Who we are

Wave Player is a music and video player app for Android, developed by SAMSAF ("we").
Contact: supportwaveplayer@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, no tracking tools, no analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • We do not run a server that stores your data. Your data stays on your phone.

3. What is stored on your phone only

The app stores on your phone: playlists, favorites, listening statistics, your settings (theme, language, sort order, etc.), the playback position of each video, hidden and recently deleted items, and what the home screen widgets need (song title, artist, and a small cover image). This data never leaves your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Permissions and why we ask

  • Read audio and video: to show and play your music and videos.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls and suggestion / new-media notifications. You can turn these off in the app settings.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep playing when the screen is off.
  • Internet: for the features described in section 5.
  • Run at phone startup: to restore the daily suggestion notification scheduled on your phone after it restarts.
  • Knowing which apps are installed: to show shortcuts for well-known apps if they are installed, and to list your apps when you tap "+" so you can pick from them. This happens on your phone and the list is not sent anywhere.

5. What leaves your phone

a) "Contact us" form: only when you press Submit, the app sends to Google Forms: the category you chose, your message text, the phone maker and model, the Android version, the app version, and an internal token. We do not ask for your name or email. It is stored in the developer's Google account and Google's policies apply. Please do not put personal information in your message.

b) Lyrics: when looking up lyrics, the app sends the song title and artist name to the free service lrclib.net. We do not send the duration, album, or any identifier. Like any website, the service sees your IP address.

c) YouTube video in the song cover: you pick the video yourself, and the official embedded YouTube player connects to YouTube/Google servers. The embedded player is a YouTube service, and by using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We store on your phone only the ID of the video you picked for each song.

d) In-app browser: websites you open (such as YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, SoundCloud and X) have their own policies and cookies. We do not collect what you do on them.

e) Sharing: when you share a file, it is sent through the app you choose.

6. Backup

The app saves a backup file, at your request, to the location you choose. It is not uploaded to any server.

7. What we do not do

We do not sell data, show ads, track your activity, or build a profile about you.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Deletion and your rights

App data is on your phone, so you delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. For "Contact us" messages, since we do not collect names or emails, we cannot link a message to a person. If you want a specific message deleted, email us quoting its text and we will delete it.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the last-updated date is shown at the top. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept it.
